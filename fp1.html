Business loans

Funding designed around business priorities.

A business loan can be used to support a wide range of commercial activities, depending on the lender, the business profile and the purpose of the finance. At FP1 Finance, we help businesses review whether this type of funding fits their need and how it may be structured appropriately.

  • Working capital to support trading, seasonal fluctuations and operating expenses.
  • Investing in growth, expansion, marketing, recruitment or new business opportunities.
  • Purchasing stock, inventory and essential goods for business continuity.
  • Refurbishment, fit-out, property improvements or business upgrades.
  • Business acquisition, strategic investment or other approved business purposes.

What can a business loan be used for?

Common uses can include:

  • Working capital and daily cash flow management
  • Expansion, hiring and new market entry
  • Stock, inventory and supplier commitments
  • Business refurbishment and infrastructure
  • Marketing campaigns and customer acquisition
  • Other approved business purposes subject to lender criteria

Eligibility, terms, rates and loan amounts depend on the lender, the applicant circumstances and the purpose of the finance. The right structure depends on the business and its commercial needs.

Asset finance

Finance the assets that help your business perform.

Asset finance enables businesses to acquire the equipment, vehicles or technology they need without necessarily paying the full purchase price upfront. This may help preserve cash flow and spread the cost over an agreed term.

  • Machinery and production equipment
  • Commercial vehicles and fleet assets
  • Technology, office equipment and specialist systems
  • Construction, agricultural and manufacturing assets

Typical asset categories

  • Plant and machinery
  • Commercial vehicles and vans
  • Construction and industrial equipment
  • Medical and office equipment
  • Technology and essential commercial systems
  • Agricultural and specialist business assets

Common structures may include hire purchase, finance lease, operating lease or other arrangements depending on the asset type and business requirements. Availability varies by lender and finance structure.

FAQs

Common questions from business owners.

What is a business loan?

A business loan is a form of commercial finance designed to support a business with funding for approved purposes, subject to lender criteria and the applicant profile.

What can a business loan be used for?

Business loans may be used for working capital, expansion, stock, refurbishment, recruitment, marketing and other approved business purposes depending on the lender and circumstances.

What is asset finance?

Asset finance allows a business to acquire essential equipment, plant, vehicles or technology and spread the cost over an agreed term, subject to approval and product availability.

What assets can be financed?

Depending on the lender and the product, assets may include machinery, commercial vehicles, office equipment, technology, medical equipment, agricultural or specialist business assets.

How much can my business borrow?

Borrowing capacity depends on the lender, business profile, trading history, affordability and purpose of the finance. There is no single amount that applies to every business.

What affects the interest rate?

Relevant factors typically include the type of finance, amount, term, asset or business profile, risk assessment and lender criteria. Actual rates vary by provider and application.

How long can business finance run?

Terms depend on the product, lender and business circumstances. The term will be discussed with the lender and may vary considerably by asset or funding type.

Can start-ups apply?

In some cases, businesses with limited trading history may still be considered, although eligibility depends on the lender, the business profile and the nature of the finance request.

What information do I need to provide?

Typically, businesses may be asked for information such as trading details, ownership structure, recent accounts, cash flow information, and the purpose of the funding. Requirements vary by application and lender.

Will applying affect my credit score?

Credit checks and affordability assessments may form part of the lender review process. The exact impact depends on the application and lender process.

How long does the application process take?

Timescales vary depending on the product, complexity and lender process. We can help businesses prepare documentation and understand likely steps in the process.

Can I repay finance early?

Early repayment may be permitted in some cases, but terms and fees depend on the lender and product. It is important to check the agreement before proceeding.

Do I need security?

Security requirements vary by product, lender and applicant profile. Some forms of finance may require security or personal guarantees, depending on the arrangement.

Do you work with multiple lenders?

Where relevant, we assess suitable finance options and may approach lenders based on the business requirement and the circumstances of the application.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?

We review the enquiry details, discuss the business requirement and determine the most relevant next steps, including whether further information or lender assessment is appropriate.
